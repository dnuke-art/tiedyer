Tie Dyer support

Tie Dyer is a tie-dye simulator: fold or twist a cloth, add bands, squirt dye, and see the unfolded pattern. It runs on the web at dnuke-art.github.io/tiedyer and as an iOS app.

Getting started

  • Tap Kikko or Spiral to load a demo. On a phone, open the controls with the menu button at top left.
  • With the Dye tool, tap or drag on the folded bundle to squirt dye. The unfolded pattern updates as it soaks through.
  • Tap 3D to orbit the bundle and dye it from any side. Band adds a rubber band that blocks the dye.
  • Save exports the plan, Image exports the pattern, both through the share sheet.
