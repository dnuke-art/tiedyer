Tie Dyer privacy policy

Last updated 15 September 2026.

The Tie Dyer app collects no data. It has no account, makes no network requests, and contains no analytics, advertising or third-party SDKs. Plans you save and images you export leave the device only when you choose to share them through the system share sheet, and they go only where you send them.

The web version at dnuke-art.github.io/tiedyer counts page views with Umami, a cookieless analytics service that records no personal information and does not track you across sites. The iOS app does not include this.

Questions: dan@dnuke.com.
