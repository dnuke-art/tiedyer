Answers to Guideline 2.1 "Information Needed":

1. Screen recording: attached (App Review Attachments), from a physical device, starting at the Home Screen. It shows the flow in item 3. The app has no account registration, login, account deletion, shared user-generated content, paid content, subscription or in-app purchase, so none of those flows exist to record.

2. Purpose and audience: Tie Dyer is a tie-dye simulator for iPhone and iPad. The user folds or twists a virtual cloth, ties rubber bands, squirts dye onto the folded bundle, and sees the resulting pattern on the unfolded cloth. Folds are real geometry with every layer tracked; the dye soaks through the layers, diffuses and fixes. It is for people who tie-dye real shirts and want to plan a pattern first, plus crafters and teachers. General audience; a creative planning tool, not a game.

3. Setup and access: no account, sign-in, sample files or hardware needed; the app opens with a demo already computed. Typical flow (about a minute): (a) open the panel with ☰ at top left and pick Kikko or Spiral from Presets; (b) with Dye selected in the switch at the top right of the bundle view, tap or drag on the bundle to squirt dye, and the unfolded pattern updates; (c) tap 3D in that corner, choose Orbit and drag to turn the bundle (pinch to zoom), then choose Dye and touch it from any side; (d) choose Band, then tap two points on the bundle to tie a band across it, which blocks the dye; (e) Save exports the plan, and Image (PNG) in the Export section at the bottom of the panel exports the pattern, both through the share sheet. The Batch section runs the diffusion and pauses by itself when the dye has fixed.

4. External services: none. All geometry, cloth and dye simulation runs on-device in the app's own code (WebGL in the app's web view). No data providers, authentication, payment processors, analytics, advertising, third-party SDKs or AI/ML services; no network requests. The only system integration is the share sheet.

5. Regional differences: none; one English localization.

6. Regulated industry / third-party material: not applicable; every pattern is generated by the simulation.
