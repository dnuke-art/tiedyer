Answers to App Review's standard Guideline 2.1 "Information Needed" questions:

1. Screen recording: attached to these review notes (App Review Attachments), captured on a physical device running the current iOS, starting from the Home Screen and app launch. It shows the typical flow described in item 3: loading the demos, dyeing the folded bundle, the 3D view, adding a rubber band, and exporting through the share sheet. The app has no account registration, login, account deletion, user-generated content that is shared or published, paid content, subscription, or in-app purchase, so none of those flows exist to record.

2. Purpose and target audience: Tie Dyer is a tie-dye simulator for iPhone and iPad (Universal build). The user folds or twists a virtual piece of cloth, places rubber bands, squirts dye onto the folded bundle, and sees the resulting pattern on the unfolded cloth. The folds are real geometry with every layer tracked, and the dye is simulated soaking through the layers, diffusing and fixing. It is for people who tie-dye real shirts and want to plan a pattern before using cloth and dye, plus crafters, teachers and anyone curious about how tie-dye patterns come from folds. General audience; no age-restricted content. It is a creative planning tool, not a game.

3. Setup and access: No account, sign-in, credentials, sample files or hardware are required. The app opens with a demo pattern already computed. Typical flow (about a minute): (a) open the side panel with the ☰ button at top left and pick Kikko or Spiral from the Presets menu to load a demo; (b) with the Dye tool selected, tap or drag on the folded bundle (lower view) to squirt dye; the unfolded pattern (upper view) updates as the dye soaks through; (c) tap 3D in the top right corner of the bundle to switch to the 3D view, drag to orbit and pinch to zoom, tap Dye and touch the bundle to squirt from any side; (d) tap Band and drag on the bundle to add a rubber band, which blocks the dye; (e) Save exports the plan as a file, and Image (PNG) in the Export section at the bottom of the panel exports the pattern, both through the system share sheet. The Batch section runs the diffusion; it pauses on its own when the dye has fixed.

4. External services: None. Tie Dyer is fully self-contained and works offline. The fold geometry, cloth simulation, dye wicking and diffusion are all computed on-device by the app's own code (WebGL in the app's web view). It uses no data providers, authentication, payment processors, analytics, advertising, third-party SDKs that phone home, or AI/ML services, and makes no network requests. The only system integration is the share sheet used for export.

5. Regional differences: None. The app functions identically in every region, with a single English localization.

6. Regulated industry / protected material: Not applicable. Tie Dyer includes no third-party content; all patterns are generated by the simulation.

Privacy: no data is collected or transmitted (consistent with the App Privacy answer "No, we do not collect data"). Support: https://dnuke-art.github.io/tiedyer/support.html · Privacy policy: https://dnuke-art.github.io/tiedyer/privacy.html.
