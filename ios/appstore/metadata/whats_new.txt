A roomier, simpler layout, especially on iPhone.

MORE ROOM FOR THE CLOTH
• The controls are a drawer: tap ☰ to slide them in and out, so the two views always have the whole screen
• The views run edge to edge, and the status bar along the bottom is gone
• The folded and unfolded views stay the same size when you rotate the phone; drag the bar between them to give one more room, and double-tap it to even them out
• The view titles are gone and every slider sits on one line with its label
• The explanations moved out of the panel into Help, which now has a guide to every control

SIMPLER CONTROLS
• One switch on the bundle view chooses what a touch does: Dye, Band, Fold (draw your own fold line) or Orbit (Inspect in 2D), with the current dye colour under it; tap the colour to change it
• A bigger ☰ button, easier to find and tap
• Kikko, Spiral and Bleach are in a Presets menu
• Image and 3D model export have their own Export section at the bottom of the panel
• Accordion, zigzag and diagonal folds are grouped under Regular folds, and the fold list folds away under a Folds heading
• Tap 3D again to fit the bundle back in view; pinch or scroll to zoom

RUBBER BANDS THAT WORK LIKE REAL ONES
• Tie a band by pressing on the bundle, dragging to the angle you want, and letting go
• A band goes straight across the whole bundle and squeezes every layer, leaving a clean line in the pattern
• Band width and Undo band are new; bands are shaded on the cloth where they cross it, in 2D and on the 3D model

ALSO
• The cloth colour and its strength (now called Base dye) are under Cloth
• A small readout in the corner of the bundle shows the layer count, how deep the last squirt reached, and what is under your finger
• The simulation runs on the GPU on more devices
