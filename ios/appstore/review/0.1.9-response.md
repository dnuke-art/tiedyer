# Resubmission with build 0.1.9: layout and paid content

Written 2026-09-21. The previous submission came back with two concerns:

- **Guideline 4.0 (Design), layout.** "Parts of the app's user interface were
  crowded, laid out, or displayed in a way that made it difficult to use the
  app when reviewed on iPhone 17 Pro Max running iOS 27.0. Specifically, we
  were unable to properly see the bottom side of the screen."
- **Paid content.** A question about paid content. The app has none.

Build 0.1.9 (tag `ios-v0.1.9`, uploaded to TestFlight 2026-09-21) fixes the
layout. The paid-content answer is a statement, now at the top of the review
notes.

## What changed for the layout concern

The cause, found in the iPhone 17 Pro Max simulator: the status line along
the bottom of the screen was about 550 pt wide on a 440 pt screen, so its end
ran off the edge. In landscape, a 300 pt docked panel also squeezed both views.

| Before (0.1.2) | Build 0.1.9 |
|---|---|
| Status line across the bottom, wider than the screen | Gone. Its information is a small readout inside the bundle view, placed clear of the home indicator |
| Control panel docked on wider screens, squeezing the views | Panel is a drawer at every size, opened with ☰ at top left; the views always have the whole screen |
| 8 pt gutters, side safe areas reserved, view title bars | Views run edge to edge on phones; titles removed; only the corner buttons and readout are kept clear of the Dynamic Island and home indicator |
| Split between the views changed on rotation | Split set by the space available, the same in both orientations, adjustable by dragging the bar between them (double-tap to even them out) |
| Explanatory paragraphs in the panel; sliders wrapping onto two lines | Explanations moved to Help; every slider is on one line |
| Tool buttons spread through the panel | One Dye / Band / Fold / Orbit switch on the bundle view, with the current dye colour under it; presets in a menu; ready-made folds grouped; export in its own section |
| 36 × 32 pt ☰ button | 44 × 44 pt, Apple's minimum touch target |
| Top of the screen and the Help screen could sit under the clock until the app had started | Safe areas apply from the first paint; Help stays clear of the clock and the home indicator |
| Layout drawn side by side for a moment at launch, then stacked | Arrangement decided in CSS, correct from the first paint |
| Double tap zoomed the page | Page zoom off in the app; no double-tap zoom |

## Screen recording for 0.1.9

On the iPhone (the layout complaint was about a phone), TestFlight build 0.1.9,
latest iOS, 60 to 90 seconds, one take. Settings → Control Center → add
*Screen Recording* if it is missing. Start recording from Control Center, then
go to the Home Screen so the recording starts **before** the app opens.

Before recording, get the phone to what a reviewer sees: delete Tie Dyer and
reinstall it from TestFlight (that clears the saved plan and settings), or at
least double-tap the bar between the views to even the split. Check first that
step 4 works on the phone: the colour sheet opening was fixed in 0.1.7 but only
checked in a browser. If it does not open, leave step 4 out.

1. Tap the Tie Dyer icon on the Home Screen. Let the demo appear. Hold a
   second on the full screen: both views edge to edge, nothing cut off at the
   bottom.
2. Tap ☰ at top left. The panel slides in. Pick **Presets → Kikko**. Tap
   the views (or ☰) to close the panel.
3. With **Dye** lit in the switch at top right of the bundle view, drag
   across the bundle. The unfolded pattern updates.
4. Tap the colour swatch under the switch: the iOS colour sheet opens; pick a
   colour and close it.
5. Tap **Band**, press on the bundle and drag to set the angle (it shows
   orange), then let go. A band is tied straight across. Tap **Dye** and dye next to the band to
   show the resist line on the unfolded cloth.
6. Tap **3D**, then **Orbit**, drag to turn the bundle, pinch to zoom. Tap
   **Dye** and touch the bundle from the side.
7. Rotate the phone to landscape and back, to show the layout holds.
8. Open ☰ again, scroll to **Export** at the bottom, tap **Image (PNG)**. The
   share sheet opens; dismiss it. Tap **Help** at the top of the panel, scroll
   a little, close it.
9. Stop recording.

No login, purchase, subscription or account flow exists, so there is nothing
else to show.

Get the file to this Mac: Photos → the recording → Share → **Tailscale** →
this machine, then `sudo tailscale file get --conflict=rename ~/Downloads`
(or AirDrop). Transcode to H.264 (HEVC from iOS is ~10× larger and not every
reviewer tool plays it):

```sh
ffmpeg -y -i ~/Downloads/<recording>.mov \
  -vf "scale='min(1366,iw)':-2:flags=lanczos,fps=30" \
  -c:v libx264 -preset slow -crf 23 -pix_fmt yuv420p -movflags +faststart -an \
  ~/Downloads/tiedyer-0.1.9-review.mp4
```

Then upload `tiedyer-0.1.9-review.mp4` in App Store Connect → the version →
App Review Information → Attachment, removing the old one (or run
`attach-recording.sh` on the workstation that has the App Store Connect tools).

## Resolution Center reply (paste this)

---

Hello, and thank you for the review.

**Layout on iPhone 17 Pro Max.** We reproduced the problem at the iPhone 17
Pro Max screen size: a status line along the bottom of the screen was wider than the
display, so its end ran off the edge, and in landscape the control panel
crowded the two views. Build 0.1.9 changes the layout throughout:

- The control panel is now a drawer, opened with the ☰ button at the top
  left, so the folded and unfolded views always have the whole screen.
- The status line is gone. Its information is a small readout inside the
  bundle view, positioned clear of the home indicator.
- The views run edge to edge. View titles and explanatory text were removed
  from the main screen; the explanations are on the Help screen.
- Every slider fits on one line with its label. The main actions (Dye, Band, Fold, Orbit) are
  one switch on the bundle view, and presets and export each have their own
  menu or section.
- The layout keeps the same proportions in portrait and landscape, and is
  correct from the first frame: nothing sits under the clock or home
  indicator, including the Help screen.

**Paid content.** Tie Dyer has no paid content. It is free, and everything
in it is free: there are no in-app purchases, subscriptions, paid unlocks,
paid tiers, tips, advertising, or links to purchase anything, inside or
outside the app. Every feature, preset and export is available to every user
from the first launch, and the app makes no network requests. This is also
stated at the top of the App Review notes.

The review notes and the attached screen recording are updated for build
0.1.9. Please let me know if anything else would help.

Dan Newcome
