## 1. Record (iPad or iPhone, latest iOS, 60–90 s)

Settings → Control Center → add *Screen Recording* if it is not there. Then
open Control Center, tap the record button, wait for the 3-second countdown,
and go to the Home Screen so the recording starts **before** the app launches.

Shot list (matches item 3 of the review notes):

1. Tap the Tie Dyer icon on the Home Screen. Let the demo pattern appear.
2. Side panel: tap **Kikko**, then **Spiral** (on iPhone open the panel with
   the menu button at top left first). Each loads a demo bundle and pattern.
3. With **Dye** selected, tap and drag on the folded bundle (lower view).
   Show the unfolded pattern (upper view) updating as dye soaks through.
4. Tap **3D** above the bundle. Drag to orbit, pinch to zoom, then tap
   **Dye** and touch the bundle from another side.
5. Tap **Band**, drag across the bundle to add a rubber band, then dye next
   to it so the band's resist shows.
6. Tap **Image**. The share sheet opens; tap *Save Image* and cancel or
   dismiss. Optionally tap **Save** to show the plan export.
7. Stop recording from the status bar / Control Center.

No login, sign-up, account deletion, purchase or user-generated-content flow
exists, so there is nothing else to show. Keep the whole thing in one take.

## 3. Transcode, attach, update the notes

```sh
ios/appstore/attach-recording.sh ~/Downloads/<recording>.mov
```

This re-encodes to H.264 at 30 fps (HEVC from iOS is ~10× larger and not
every reviewer tool plays it), uploads it as an App Review attachment on
the 0.1.2 review detail, and rewrites review-notes item 1 in App Store
Connect to say the recording is attached. Re-run with a new file to replace.

Purpose and audience. Tie Dyer is a tie-dye simulator. The user folds or
twists a virtual piece of cloth, places rubber bands, squirts dye onto the
folded bundle and sees the resulting pattern on the unfolded cloth. Every
fold layer is tracked and the dye is simulated soaking through, diffusing and
fixing. It is for people who tie-dye real garments and want to plan a pattern
before committing cloth and dye, plus crafters, teachers and anyone curious
about how the patterns come from the folds. General audience; it is a
planning tool, not a game.

Setup and access. No account, sign-in, credentials, sample files or hardware
are needed. The app opens with a demo pattern already computed and is fully
usable offline.

External services. None. All geometry, cloth and dye simulation runs
on-device in the app's own code. There are no data providers,
authentication, payments, analytics, advertising, third-party SDKs or AI/ML
services, and the app makes no network requests. The only system integration
is the share sheet for export.

Regional differences. None; identical in every region, one English
localization.

Regulated industry / third-party material. Not applicable. The app contains
no third-party content; every pattern is generated by the simulation.
