# Resubmission with build 0.1.6: layout and paid content

Written 2026-09-21. The previous submission came back with two concerns:

- **Guideline 4.0 (Design), layout.** "Parts of the app's user interface were
  crowded, laid out, or displayed in a way that made it difficult to use the
  app when reviewed on iPhone 17 Pro Max running iOS 27.0. Specifically, we
  were unable to properly see the bottom side of the screen."
- **Paid content.** A question about paid content. The app has none.

Build 0.1.6 (tag `ios-v0.1.6`, uploaded to TestFlight 2026-09-21) fixes the
layout. The paid-content answer is a statement, now at the top of the review
notes.

## What changed for the layout concern

The cause, found in the iPhone 17 Pro Max simulator: the status line along
the bottom of the screen was about 550 pt wide on a 440 pt screen, so its end
ran off the edge. In landscape, a 300 pt docked panel also squeezed both views.

| Before (0.1.2) | Build 0.1.6 |
|---|---|
| Status line across the bottom, wider than the screen | Gone. Its information is a small readout inside the bundle view, placed clear of the home indicator |
| Control panel docked on wider screens, squeezing the views | Panel is a drawer at every size, opened with ☰ at top left; the views always have the whole screen |
| 8 pt gutters, side safe areas reserved, view title bars | Views run edge to edge on phones; titles removed; only the corner buttons and readout are kept clear of the Dynamic Island and home indicator |
| Split between the views changed on rotation | Split set by the space available, the same in both orientations, adjustable by a drag bar or the Split slider |
| Explanatory paragraphs in the panel; sliders wrapping onto two lines | Explanations moved to Help; every slider is on one line |
| Tool buttons spread through the panel | One Dye / Band / Orbit switch on the bundle view; presets in a menu; export in its own section |

## Resolution Center reply (paste this)

---

Hello, and thank you for the review.

**Layout on iPhone 17 Pro Max.** We reproduced the problem at the iPhone 17
Pro Max screen size: a status line along the bottom of the screen was wider than the
display, so its end ran off the edge, and in landscape the control panel
crowded the two views. Build 0.1.6 changes the layout throughout:

- The control panel is now a drawer, opened with the ☰ button at the top
  left, so the folded and unfolded views always have the whole screen.
- The status line is gone. Its information is a small readout inside the
  bundle view, positioned clear of the home indicator.
- The views run edge to edge. View titles and explanatory text were removed
  from the main screen; the explanations are on the Help screen.
- Every slider fits on one line with its label. The main actions (Dye, Band, Orbit) are
  one switch on the bundle view, and presets and export each have their own
  menu or section.
- The layout keeps the same proportions in portrait and landscape.

**Paid content.** Tie Dyer has no paid content. It is free, and everything
in it is free: there are no in-app purchases, subscriptions, paid unlocks,
paid tiers, tips, advertising, or links to purchase anything, inside or
outside the app. Every feature, preset and export is available to every user
from the first launch, and the app makes no network requests. This is also
stated at the top of the App Review notes.

The review notes and the attached screen recording are updated for build
0.1.6. Please let me know if anything else would help.

Dan Newcome
