How Tie Dyer works

The two views

The flat cloth is the shirt unfolded: the pattern you will get. The folded bundle is what you actually squirt. Everything you do to the bundle shows up on the flat cloth at once, and hovering either view shows where that point sits in the other.

Fold, then bind, then dye

Use the Shape section to fold: accordion pleats, zigzag triangles, diagonals, or draw your own fold line (click a point, move to set the angle, click, then click the side that moves; the creases it would make are previewed on the flat cloth as you go). Twist mode pinches the centre and winds the cloth into a spiral instead. Bands and clamps squeeze the layers so dye cannot get in.

Squirting is wicking

A squirt pours liquid onto the bundle and it wicks through the layers right away, top layer first, then the next, until the liquid is used up. Soak is how much liquid one squirt puts out, in layers: soak 1 fills the top layer under the nozzle, soak 10 reaches ten layers down. The dye goes wherever the liquid goes. This decides the reach of every squirt before any diffusion happens, which is also how it works with a real squeeze bottle. In a thick stack, sideways wick is the lever for depth: every layer's neighbours take a share on the way down, so turn it down to send a squirt straight through, and watch the status line, which reports how many layers the last squirt reached.

Working a spot

  • Hold the button still and the squirt keeps pouring: its soak grows every second (hold flow), so the front moves deeper while you wait. This is the way to reach layers one squirt cannot. Set hold flow to off for pure click-and-stamp painting.
  • Go over an area again and it gets darker, up to the build-up limit, then stops. The cloth remembers it is wet: a full spot takes no more liquid, extra liquid pushes deeper, and a new colour on a wet spot mixes in. Build-up 0 means a colour can never get stronger than one squirt.
  • Whatever you did is saved as strokes. Rewind, Load and every fold change replay them exactly.

Batch: let it sit

▶ Play runs the hours in the bag. Free dye spreads within a layer, bleeds thru layers where they touch, and fixes to the fibres at the fixing rate until the cloth's capacity is used. Fixed dye is what survives the rinse; tick Rinse to see only that. Play pauses itself when nothing is left to move.

Bleach

The BL swatch squirts bleach instead of dye. It wicks and spreads like dye but removes colour wherever it reaches, fixed or not, and fades as it works (bleach power, bleach fade). Pick a cloth colour to start from a solid shirt and discharge a pattern out of it. Free bleach is tinted pale blue so you can see it on white cloth.

3D

Switch the bundle to 3D to orbit it and squirt from any side, including the edges of a stack. Paint on: a drag squirts or bands. Paint off: a drag orbits. Right-drag always orbits; two fingers pan, and pinch zooms.

Folds work in 3D too: with Draw fold line on, tap the bundle (or the table around it) for the two points and the side, and drag to orbit in between. The crease is always a line on the table, shown as a plane cutting through the stack, with the part that will fold over tinted.

Layer height (View section) sets how thick each layer is drawn, from 0.1 mm to 1 cm. Tall stacks from many folds can be squashed flat to see the whole shape, or stretched to paint the edges. It is saved with the plan, since it also sets how much of each layer's edge a side squirt can reach.

Image saves the unfolded cloth as a PNG at the size chosen under View (1024, 2048 or 4096 px on the long side), with creases if they are shown. The detail in it comes from the resolution setting under Cloth: 240 texels is a 2.5 mm texel on a 60 cm cloth, 800 is 0.75 mm. Set the resolution before dyeing; changing it later replays the strokes on the new grid.

GLB saves the bundle as a 3D model (glTF binary) with the dye as its texture, for Blender, a web viewer, or AR Quick Look on iOS. Rinse first if you want only fixed dye in the texture.

Mouse and keys

Drag to squirt or band. Hold still to keep pouring. space play/pause · z undo stroke · esc cancel a fold line · ? this help. The ☰ button hides and shows the controls (on a phone they slide in over the views).

Drag the bar between the two views, or use split under View, to give one of them more room; double-click the bar to even them out. The views sit side by side when there is more width than height and stack otherwise, and keep the same split when you rotate the device.
